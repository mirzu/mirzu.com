Jabba's Barge went to the playa in 2011 and was a much more ambitious project than Sakana — and the most successful from a fun perspective.

Built from steel on a Ford F-450, we modified it to be driven from the platform on top, and eventually added fire. It also had its own sound system so we could host parties.
